Precise, presentation-ready 3D printed scale models for architects, engineering firms, and real estate developers in Kathmandu Valley.

Built to Match Your Drawings

Site plans, massing studies, and detailed presentation models all depend on accurate scale — a model that doesn't match the drawings undermines the pitch it's meant to support. We 3D print from your CAD, SketchUp, or Revit export, checking footprint and proportions against your original file before anything goes to print.

Most architectural models are printed in PLA for a clean, paintable finish, with finer detail work available in resin where a project calls for it. We can print terrain, building massing, and separable floor plates depending on how you plan to present the model.

Typical clients are architecture practices, engineering consultancies, and developers preparing models for client meetings, planning submissions, or sales offices in Kathmandu, Lalitpur, and Bhaktapur.

What We Need From You

  • A 3D file (STL/OBJ) or CAD drawings of the building or site
  • Your intended scale (e.g. 1:100, 1:200, 1:500)
  • Deadline (e.g. a client presentation or submission date)
  • Any finishing preference — raw print, painted, or with base/plinth

FAQ

What scale do you print architectural models at?
We work to whatever scale your drawings use — common choices are 1:100, 1:200, and 1:500 for site and massing models. Tell us the scale and building footprint and we'll confirm what's printable.
Can you print from my CAD or Revit file?
Yes. Export your model as STL or OBJ (most CAD, SketchUp, and Revit workflows can do this) and send it over WhatsApp. If you only have 2D drawings, tell us and we can discuss options.
How long does an architectural model take?
It depends on the size and detail of the building — we confirm a specific timeline once we see the file or drawings, as part of the quote.
