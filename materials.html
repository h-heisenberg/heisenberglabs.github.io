3D Printing Materials Guide

We primarily print in PLA and PETG, with resin available for high-detail work. Here's how to choose.

PLA (Polylactic Acid)

Our default material for models, gifts, and prototypes. Easy to print cleanly, takes color and paint well, and holds fine detail — best for parts that won't see heavy heat or stress.

Good for: architectural models, figurines, keychains, display pieces, prototypes

PETG

Tougher and more heat- and impact-resistant than PLA, with good outdoor durability. Our go-to for parts that get handled, mounted outside, or need to survive daily use.

Good for: bike/car accessories, electronics enclosures, functional parts

Resin

Cured layer by layer under UV light for much finer detail and a smoother surface than filament printing — at a higher cost and slower speed.

Good for: fine-detail figurines, lithophane test pieces, jewelry-scale work

FDM vs Resin Printing

FDM (Filament — PLA/PETG)

  • Lower cost, faster for larger parts
  • Good for functional, everyday-use parts
  • Visible layer lines at close inspection

Resin (SLA)

  • Much finer detail and smoother finish
  • Best for small, detailed pieces
  • Higher cost, more post-processing
