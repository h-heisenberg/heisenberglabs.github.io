A Gift Made From Your Idea or Photo

Our most popular personalized gift is the lithophane lamp — a thin 3D printed panel that reveals a photo when lit from behind, made from any picture you send us. We also print custom keychains, small figurines, name plates, and trophies for events, farewells, and recognitions.

Around Dashain and Tihar we take bulk orders for festival and corporate gifting — the earlier you reach out before the festival, the more comfortably we can fit your order into the print schedule.

Most gifts are printed in PLA, which takes color and detail well and is well suited to display pieces that won't see rough daily handling. We'll suggest resin instead where a design needs finer detail than PLA can hold.

We're building a photo library of completed gifts. See the kinds of projects we take on in our portfolio, or message us on WhatsApp for examples similar to your idea.

How Much Does a Personalized 3D Printed Gift Cost?

Small gifts like keychains use very little material, so cost is mostly driven by print time and finishing rather than material alone — PLA is billed at Rs. 10 per gram. A lithophane lamp or custom figurine that needs a design built from your photo carries a design fee starting at Rs. 1,500, revised by size and complexity; simple keychains with a name or initials usually don't need a separate design fee. Bulk corporate or festival orders are quoted per unit.

FAQ

What is a lithophane lamp?
A lithophane is a thin 3D printed panel where a photo appears in the plastic itself, becoming visible when backlit by a lamp. It's a popular personalized gift made from a photo you send us.
Can you do bulk corporate gifting or Dashain-Tihar orders?
Yes, we take bulk orders for corporate gifting and festival season. Reach out ahead of Dashain and Tihar with your quantity and timeline so we can plan the print schedule.
Can I get a figurine made from a photo?
We can design simple custom figurines and keychains based on photos or a description — send us references over WhatsApp and we'll confirm what's achievable.
