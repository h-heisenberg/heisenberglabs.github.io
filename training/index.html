Heisenberg Labs runs hands-on 3D printing training workshops in Kathmandu Valley for schools and colleges, covering how 3D printing works, common materials, and a live print demonstration. Learn How a Print Actually Gets Made

Our training workshops walk students through the fundamentals of 3D printing — how a digital design becomes a physical object, what the common materials are good for, and the hands-on basics of preparing and running a print. Sessions are built for school and college groups in Kathmandu Valley, with the pace adjusted to the audience.

A typical workshop covers the 3D printing process end to end, a look at PLA and PETG materials, and a hands-on segment where students see a print run and, where practical, try simple design tools themselves.

We can run a session at our workshop in Kalanki or discuss visiting your school or college, depending on group size and equipment needs.

We're building a photo library from past training workshops. See the kinds of projects we take on in our portfolio, or message us on WhatsApp for more detail on past sessions.

How Much Does a 3D Printing Workshop Cost?

Workshop pricing depends on group size, session length, and whether we travel to your school or college or host at our Kalanki workshop. Tell us your group size, preferred location, and dates, and we'll confirm a quote. There's no separate material charge unless students take home a printed piece, in which case it's billed at our standard material rates — Rs. 10 per gram for PLA, Rs. 14 per gram for PETG.
