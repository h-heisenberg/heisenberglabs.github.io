About Heisenberg Labs

A 3D printing studio in Kalanki, Kathmandu, built around fast turnaround and prints that fit their purpose the first time.

Who We Are

Heisenberg Labs is a 3D printing service based in Kalanki, Kathmandu. We work with architects and engineering firms on scale models, with startups and repair shops on electronics enclosures and prototypes, and with individuals across Nepal on personalized gifts and everyday accessories.

We also run hands-on 3D printing training workshops for schools and colleges in Kathmandu Valley, because we think understanding how a print is made makes it easier to design something that prints well.

What We Focus On

Five services, chosen deliberately rather than "we print anything."

Precision Over Guesswork

Architectural models and electronics enclosures need to match real dimensions — we check fit before we call a job done.

Safety-Conscious Scope

For bike and car accessories we stick to cosmetic and convenience parts — mounts, clips, holders, organizers. We do not print brake, steering, or engine components.

Kathmandu Valley, Nepal-wide

Based in Kalanki for walk-ins and pickup, with delivery arranged across Kathmandu, Lalitpur, Bhaktapur, and the rest of Nepal.
