Frequently Asked Questions

Where can I get 3D printing done in Kathmandu?
Heisenberg Labs, based in Kalanki, Kathmandu, offers professional 3D printing for architectural models, electronics enclosures, bike and car accessories, personalized gifts, and training workshops, with delivery across Nepal.
What can Heisenberg Labs 3D print?
Architectural scale models, custom electronics enclosures and prototypes, non-safety-critical bike and car accessories, personalized gifts such as figurines, lithophane lamps and keychains, and 3D printing training workshops for schools and colleges.
How do I get a quote?
Message us on WhatsApp with your idea, sketch, or 3D file and we'll reply with a quote and timeline. You can also use the contact form on our Contact page.
Which materials do you print with?
We primarily print with PLA and PETG, and offer resin printing for high-detail parts. See our Materials page for a full comparison.
